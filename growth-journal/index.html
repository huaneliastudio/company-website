Growth Journal

Track progress, reflect weekly, and build your knowledge over time.

Growth Journal helps you record work progress, reading notes, learning moments, and next steps in a structured private journal. Review your days and weeks, connect related entries through your Knowledge Library, and export your progress as a readable Markdown file when needed. iCloud sync is available across Apple devices when enabled.

Designed for progress, reflection, and learning.

Growth Journal helps users capture meaningful progress, review what changed, and connect related notes over time without making the process feel complicated.

Work, Reading, and Learning Logs

Capture daily work progress, reading notes, learning takeaways, tags, and next steps in one structured journal.

Daily and Weekly Review

Look back by day or week to review what changed, what questions remain, and what actions should come next.

Weekly Reflection

Write a short weekly reflection to capture what you learned, what appeared often, and what to focus on next.

Knowledge Library

Browse entries by tags, projects, companies, books, materials, topics, and sources so related notes stay connected.

Current Understanding

Summarize what you currently understand about a topic, project, book, or tag, and update it as your thinking develops.

Private iCloud Sync and Export

Keep entries local-first, sync with your private iCloud account when available, and export your journal as a Markdown file.

Privacy

Built with privacy in mind.

Growth Journal stores journal entries on device and may sync them across Apple devices using the user's private iCloud account when iCloud is enabled.

The app does not require a separate account and Huanelia Studio does not operate a custom server for journal data.

Progress Plus

Progress Plus is available as a one-time in-app purchase for users who want deeper progress reviews, next-step tracking, focus areas, recurring themes, and Markdown export.

Guided Progress Review

Review momentum, follow-through, patterns, and priority next steps from recent logs.

Monthly Review

Summarize monthly activity across work, reading, and learning entries.

Next-Step Tracking

Track open and completed next steps so notes can turn into action.

Markdown Export

Export a readable Markdown progress report for saving, sharing, or reviewing outside the app.
