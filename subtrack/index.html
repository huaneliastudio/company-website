SubTrack+

Track renewals. Review spending. Stay organized.

SubTrack+ is a subscription tracking app designed to help users monitor recurring payments, renewal dates, monthly spending, yearly totals, and subscriptions worth reviewing. It helps users stay organized, avoid forgotten renewals, and understand their ongoing expenses more clearly.

Designed for simple subscription management.

SubTrack+ focuses on making recurring payments easier to track, review, and organize without unnecessary complexity.

Track Recurring Payments

Add subscriptions with prices, billing cycles, renewal dates, currencies, categories, icons, and optional payment methods.

Renewal Calendar

View upcoming payments in a calendar, see renewal dots, monthly totals, and payments due on each selected date.

Home Screen Widgets

Keep an eye on upcoming renewals and subscription totals from your Home Screen without opening the app.

Privacy

Built with privacy in mind.

SubTrack+ does not require an account. Your subscription tracking information is designed to stay on your device unless otherwise stated.

Payment method tracking is optional and limited to nickname, type, and optional last 4 digits. SubTrack+ does not ask for full card numbers, CVV codes, expiry dates, bank logins, or payment account passwords.

Local reminders, widgets, privacy settings, and spending insights are designed to help you stay organized while keeping the experience simple and transparent.
