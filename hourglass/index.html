Hourglass

Protect your attention before distraction takes over.

Hourglass is an iPhone focus protection app that helps users put deliberate boundaries around distracting apps. Create reusable app groups, schedule hard locks or time limits, start protection instantly with Protect Now, and review Focus History to see how consistently you stayed with your plan.

Designed for intentional phone use.

Hourglass combines scheduled protection with quick, on-demand controls so users can decide their limits before a distracting moment begins.

Reusable App Groups

Group distracting apps once, then reuse those groups across schedules and Protect Now sessions.

Hard Lock

Shield selected apps for a planned window when you want a firm boundary and fewer chances to drift back into distraction.

Shared or Per-App Time Limits

Set one allowance shared across a group or give each individually selected app its own allowance. When a limit is reached, the relevant apps become protected.

Protect Now

Start a focus protection session immediately without creating a permanent recurring schedule first.

Early-Unlock Friction

Choose Normal, Friction, or Strict behavior to add deliberate steps before ending eligible protection early.

Focus History

Review completed sessions and intentional early unlocks to understand how consistently you kept the boundaries you set.

Privacy

Built around Apple’s Screen Time controls.

Hourglass uses Apple’s Screen Time and Family Controls frameworks to apply app shielding and usage limits. App groups, schedules, preferences, and focus activity are designed to stay under the user’s control.

Hourglass does not require a separate Huanelia Studio account for its core focus protection features.

Protection that adapts to different situations.

Schedules & Week View

Create recurring protection windows and review the week visually so planned focus periods are easy to understand.

Overlapping Protection

When rules overlap, Hourglass applies the strongest effective protection for each app while preserving the underlying sessions.

Local Notifications

Receive restrained notifications for protection starts, warnings, usage-limit thresholds, and endings when permission is enabled.

Important platform limitations

Hourglass relies on Apple-provided Screen Time APIs. iOS can report when a configured usage threshold is reached, but it does not provide a continuously accurate live “minutes remaining” value for app usage allowances.

Strict mode is the strongest resistance Hourglass can provide inside the app, but an iPhone owner can still remove the app, revoke Screen Time permission, or change system settings outside Hourglass.
