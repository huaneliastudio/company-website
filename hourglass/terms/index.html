2. About Hourglass

Hourglass is a personal focus and app-protection tool that can help you organize distracting apps, create schedules, start protection manually, apply hard locks or time limits, and review your own focus activity.

3. Apple Screen Time Limitations

Hourglass depends on Apple frameworks including Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity. The app cannot override iOS itself or guarantee protection if required permissions are removed or system behavior changes.

The owner of an iPhone may still uninstall Hourglass, revoke Screen Time authorization, change device settings, or take other actions outside the app that Hourglass cannot prevent.

4. Strict Mode

Strict mode is intended to provide the strongest early-unlock resistance available inside Hourglass. It is not a device-owner lock and does not make protection impossible to bypass at the operating-system level.

5. Time Limits

Time-limit behavior depends on Apple’s Device Activity threshold reporting. Hourglass may configure a shared allowance across a selected group or, where supported, an allowance for each individually selected app. Once iOS reports a configured threshold as reached, Hourglass can apply the corresponding protection.

Apple does not provide Hourglass with a continuously accurate live value for remaining app-usage minutes, so a protection-window countdown refers to the schedule window unless explicitly stated otherwise.
