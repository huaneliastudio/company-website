Hourglass

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Hourglass handles information when you use the app.

Last updated: 11 August 2026

1. Overview

Hourglass is a focus protection app developed by Huanelia Studio. It helps users create app groups, schedule protection, start protection on demand, apply hard locks or time limits, and review focus activity. Hourglass does not require a separate Huanelia Studio account for its core features.

2. Information Stored by the App

Depending on the features you use, Hourglass may store App Group names and Apple-provided selections, protection schedules, start and end times, repeat days, modes and strictness settings, Protect Now sessions, Focus History records, early-unlock reasons you choose to enter, and app preferences.

3. Screen Time and Family Controls

Hourglass uses Apple frameworks including Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity to provide app selection, shielding, schedules, and usage-limit behavior. App and category selections are handled through Apple’s Screen Time APIs.

4. Usage Limits

For configured time limits, iOS can notify Hourglass when a Device Activity threshold has been reached. Apple does not provide Hourglass with a continuously accurate live countdown of remaining app-usage minutes, so Hourglass does not claim to provide one.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Hourglass may send local notifications about protection starts, warnings, usage-limit thresholds, and endings. Notification permission can be changed in iOS settings.

6. Data Storage

Core Hourglass configuration and focus activity are designed to be stored locally on your device unless a future app feature explicitly identifies another storage or synchronization method.

7. Data Sharing

Huanelia Studio does not sell personal data. Apple system services may process information required to provide Screen Time, Family Controls, notifications, App Store distribution, backups, or other operating-system features according to Apple’s own terms and privacy policies.

8. Early-Unlock Reasons

If you choose to write a reason for ending eligible protection early, that reason becomes part of your Hourglass focus activity. Avoid entering information you do not want stored with your app data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Hourglass changes. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when material changes are published.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact support@huaneliastudio.com.
